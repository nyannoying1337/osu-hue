osu!hue 0.4.1 — Windows x64 preview

Extract the complete ZIP and run OsuHue.exe. No separate .NET installation
is required. This is an independent community tool.
Choose English or Deutsch at the top right. Your language choice is saved.

1. Enter your own Hue Bridge IP address and click IP pruefen (IP prüfen).
2. Press the physical Bridge button, click Koppeln, then select an
   Entertainment area created in the official Hue app.
3. Click Licht-Demo to test your lights without osu! or tosu.
4. For gameplay, open osu! stable or lazer and start normal released tosu. The app's
   download button can fetch the official release. Click Licht-Sync starten
   after the preview has recognised your map.

PATCHED TOSU IS NOT REQUIRED.
The app falls back to the normal beatmap-file endpoint when the proposed
timeline endpoint is unavailable (HTTP 404).

Demo-Tempo: fixed 90/120/180/240/300 BPM or an automatic eight-second cycle.
Brightness and fade controls work in both the demo and gameplay.
Demo beenden / Licht-Sync stoppen release the lights and restore the saved
state. Normal close does too. Network/power failures may prevent restoration.

Each person pairs their own Bridge. Credentials are stored locally with
Windows DPAPI protection; no keys or settings are bundled in this ZIP.

Target: Windows x64, osu! stable or lazer; native maps in all four modes.
osu!/Catch: combo colours, including built-in lazer Argon / Argon Pro.
Taiko: Don/Kat red/blue pulses. Mania: column accents and mixed chords.
These are object-start effects: no slider ticks/repeats, drum rolls, nested
Catch fruit/droplets/bananas, hold tails or custom texture colours.
Standard-to-Taiko converts and Standard-to-Catch heads work. Mania
conversions are not supported.
Mania column-changing mods and custom texture colours are not reproduced.
See README in the source repository for the full support table.
